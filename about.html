About Us

Immaculate Day Care is a community-based non-profit organisation that provides care, early childhood education and support for young children in a safe and loving environment.


Our team is committed to creating an environment where every child feels welcome, supported and encouraged to reach their full potential.

Our Mission

To provide young children with quality care, early education and support in a safe and nurturing environment.

Our Vision

To create a positive impact in our community by helping children grow, learn and reach their full potential.
